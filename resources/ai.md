# AI, LLM & Inference

Curated AI APIs, inference providers, model routers, speech services, and AI developer tools with an official free tier, free credits, or trial access.

> Last reviewed: **2026-08-18**. Limits and model availability can change frequently.

## LLM APIs & Model Routers

### OpenRouter

Unified API for many AI models, including a rotating collection of free models.

- **Offer:** 25+ free models on the Free plan; `openrouter/free` provides zero-cost routing across available free models
- **Type:** 🆓 Free Tier
- **Card required:** No for the Free plan
- **API:** Yes
- **Official link:** https://openrouter.ai/
- **Free models:** https://openrouter.ai/models?q=free
- **Pricing:** https://openrouter.ai/pricing
- **Last verified:** 2026-08-18
- **Notes:** Free-model availability changes over time. The Free plan is rate-limited.
