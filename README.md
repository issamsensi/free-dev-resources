# Free Dev Resources

A curated collection of **free tiers, developer credits, AI APIs, cloud services, databases, developer tools, student offers, and startup programs**.

The goal is not to collect the largest number of links. The goal is to keep a useful, developer-focused list where offers are **categorized, linked to official sources, and periodically re-verified**.

> Free tiers and promotional offers change frequently. Always confirm current limits and eligibility on the provider's official page before depending on an offer.

## Offer types

- 🆓 **Free Tier** — ongoing free usage with limits
- 🎁 **Free Credits** — credits granted for a limited amount of usage
- ⏱️ **Trial** — time-limited or evaluation access
- 🎓 **Student** — requires student eligibility
- 🚀 **Startup** — requires startup eligibility
- ✅ **Verified** — checked against an official provider source
- ⚠️ **Unverified** — interesting candidate that still needs validation

## Verification principles

Resources should link to an **official pricing, documentation, product, or program page** whenever possible.

For each detailed entry we aim to record:

- What is actually free
- Whether a payment card is required
- Important usage limits or restrictions
- Whether the offer is ongoing, trial-based, student-only, or startup-only
- The official product and documentation links
- The date the offer was last verified

We intentionally avoid shady methods, fake accounts, leaked credentials, bypasses, unsupported workarounds, and abuse of free tiers.
